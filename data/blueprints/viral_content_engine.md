# 30-Day Omnichannel Viral AI Content Matrix
## The High-Converting Micro-SaaS & EdTech Playbook

### Core Framework: The 4-Pillar Hook Formula
1. The Counter-Intuitive Truth: 'Stop studying 6 hours a day for IELTS. Here is why the top 1% only study 45 minutes.'
2. The High-Value Tool Stack: '9 free AI tools so powerful they feel illegal for university students in 2026.'
3. The Step-by-Step Teardown: 'How a beginner passed IELTS Writing Task 2 with Band 8.5 using this exact prompt chain.'
4. The Cost-Benefit Shock: 'Tutors charge $40/hour to proofread essays. This $1 AI examiner gives you Cambridge-level feedback in 8 seconds.'

### Omnichannel Distribution Chain
- Step 1 (Source Anchor): Write a 1,500-word deep dive or case study.
- Step 2 (Telegram & WhatsApp Channel): Condense into a 3-bullet teaser with direct one-click link.
- Step 3 (X / Twitter Thread): Hook -> 5-step breakdown -> Visual screenshot -> CTA.
- Step 4 (LinkedIn Article): Professional framing focused on career mobility, salary boost, and ATS optimization.
- Step 5 (Short-Form Video / Reels Script): Hook (0-3s) -> Problem demonstration (3-15s) -> 1-Click Solution (15-30s) -> Link in bio.
